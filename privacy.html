ViniClips – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who uses this app

ViniClips is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no public users.

2. Data we access

With the owner's permission, ViniClips accesses only the information TikTok provides through login (basic account info) and the access token needed to upload videos as drafts to the owner's own account.

3. How data is used

This data is used only to upload the owner's videos to his TikTok account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Storage

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer and are never published.

5. Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app settings, under the list of connected apps.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
